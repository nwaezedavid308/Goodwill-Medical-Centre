# GMC — care, made clear

The user's seven references informed the proportions, expressive typography and playful card arrangement. Their palettes, illustrations and content were not copied.

Milk `#FFF9F2` remains the base. Purple `#7939C6` anchors key actions, sky `#BCE6F6` marks wayfinding, lavender `#E6DAFA` softens secondary surfaces, and ink `#241C30` provides the outlines and text. Plus Jakarta Sans is used throughout the website, including headings, cards, forms and dialogs, matching the landing page. The former Playfair Display font has been removed.

The layout pairs a large, left-aligned heading with an asymmetric three-part grid: a clear invitation, real hospital photography and two practical action cards. Medical plus signs are structural brand details. Mixed card sizes, small offset shadows on interaction and a lightly tilted insurance stack provide personality without mascots or neon.

Framer Motion adds gentle directional scroll reveals across pages, staggered card entrances from the left, right and below, hover/tap feedback and gentle scroll-linked parallax behind the hero video. Selected service cards float by just three pixels over seven seconds while visible, stopping on hover, focus and reduced-motion preference. Scroll entrances use CSS translate independently of hover transforms and replay when content re-enters the viewport. The hero headline types and rotates through caring messages, with a pause control and a stable screen-reader heading. The facility video attempts autoplay with sound, falls back to muted playback when browser policy requires it, and offers explicit sound and playback controls. It pauses offscreen. Reduced-motion visitors see a static headline and a manually playable poster instead. The original GMC SVG supplies the header, footer and favicon mark.

A sky-blue patient-support card immediately follows the hero, with matching support links in the desktop navigation, mobile menu and footer. Its button prepares a custom WhatsApp message asking how to support patients and GMC's vision. No donation amount, payment transaction or delivery confirmation is implied.

Primary tasks are explicit: request a visit, explore services, find directions and call for urgent help. The former top information bar is removed entirely. Emergency contact stays available in the contact menu and footer.

The former individual clinician directory has been replaced by a showcase of 14 clinical and support teams. Staff and department-space photos have captions describing what is pictured; support departments without supplied portraits use GMC facility imagery. Individual clinician names have also been removed from appointment requests. GMC still needs to confirm schedules, programme terms and historical impact figures. The new homepage avoids patient quotes and undated numerical impact claims.

The landing video uses a web-ready H.264 copy of BEST HOSPITAL INTRO1019.mp4 with its original audio removed. The first 40.31 seconds of the supplied Vector Pulse.mp3 replace that soundtrack, encoded as AAC in the same MP4 so playback and sound controls remain synchronized. The original uploads remain untouched locally.
