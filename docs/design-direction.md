# GMC — care, made clear

The user's seven references informed the proportions, expressive typography and playful card arrangement. Their palettes, illustrations and content were not copied.

Milk `#FFF9F2` remains the base. Purple `#7939C6` anchors key actions, sky `#BCE6F6` marks wayfinding, lavender `#E6DAFA` softens secondary surfaces, and ink `#241C30` provides the outlines and text. The existing Plus Jakarta Sans / Playfair Display families remain available; the new homepage uses the sans family for a bolder, simpler hierarchy.

The layout pairs a large, left-aligned heading with an asymmetric three-part grid: a clear invitation, real hospital photography and two practical action cards. Medical plus signs are structural brand details. Mixed card sizes, small offset shadows on interaction and a lightly tilted insurance stack provide personality without mascots, neon or animation loops.

Motion is deliberately limited to a short hero entrance, one reveal per major homepage section, and responsive controls. Reduced-motion preferences disable these effects. No video autoplays.

Primary tasks are explicit: request a visit, explore services, find directions and call for urgent help. The former top information bar is removed entirely. Emergency contact stays available in the contact menu and footer.

The former individual clinician directory has been replaced by a showcase of 14 clinical and support teams. Staff and department-space photos have captions describing what is pictured; support departments without supplied portraits use GMC facility imagery. Individual clinician names have also been removed from appointment requests. GMC still needs to confirm schedules, programme terms and historical impact figures. The new homepage avoids patient quotes and undated numerical impact claims.
